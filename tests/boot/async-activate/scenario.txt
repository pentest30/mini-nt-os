# Activating a window of another thread (win32k::input::activate,
# activate_event, deactivate_thread; win32k::queue's events, POSTEVENT_SAW or
# WM_ASYNC_SETACTIVEWINDOW, and POSTEVENT_DAW; tests/native/asyncact1.c,
# MinGW, linked to ReactOS's kernel32 and user32): ASYNCACT1.EXE takes the
# place of winlogon.exe and switches to WinSta0\Default. Its thread A shows
# A1; its thread B shows B1 (with a child BC) and B2 without activating them.
# Update with docs/compat/m6-fenetres.md, step 43.
# 1. A: SetForegroundWindow(B1). A deactivates itself at once (A1:
#    WM_NCACTIVATE, WM_ACTIVATE, WM_ACTIVATEAPP, WM_KILLFOCUS); B, in its
#    thread, activates B1 (WM_ACTIVATEAPP to B1 and B2, then B1's
#    WM_NCACTIVATE, WM_ACTIVATE, WM_SETFOCUS).
# 2. B gives the focus to BC; A: SetForegroundWindow(B2). B moves its
#    activation from B1 to B2 in its thread; no WM_ACTIVATEAPP.
# 3. While B sleeps in a handler, A: SetForegroundWindow(A1), then
#    SetForegroundWindow(B2). B's deactivation goes (QueueState::
#    remove_deactivations); B, awake: WM_ACTIVATEAPP to B1 and B2, B2's
#    WM_NCACTIVATE, nothing else (B2 still active).
# 4. While B sleeps, A: SetForegroundWindow(B1); B, awake, makes B2 active
#    itself: the activation of B1 that waited goes (QueueState::
#    remove_activations).
# 5. A: SetForegroundWindow(A1). B deactivates itself in its thread (B2:
#    WM_NCACTIVATE, WM_ACTIVATE; WM_ACTIVATEAPP to B1 and B2; B2's
#    WM_KILLFOCUS); A activates A1.
