# extra bytes SBWND), then:
# 1. SetScrollInfo and GetScrollInfo of SB_CTL (win32k::scroll, the third
#    bar);

# 6. ShowScrollBar(SB_CTL) hides then shows the control (ShowWindow);

#    control has it again (win32k::window::replace_procedure);
# 8. the same in ANSI: the class's ANSI procedure, then the server's again;
# 9. user32 paints it (WM_PAINT of the control, NtUserGetScrollBarInfo):
#    the control at (110, 110)-(130, 310) on the screen; the face and the
#    black symbol of its top arrow, its thumb at the end (position 90: its
