# Menus that user32 reads itself (win32k::menu: MENU and ITEM in the
# desktop's heap; tests/native/menu1.c, MinGW, linked to ReactOS's kernel32
# and user32): MENU1.EXE takes the place of winlogon.exe and switches to
# WinSta0\Default. It builds a bar (File, Edit, Help), gives it to W (100,
# 100, 400 x 300, white) and reads every change back through user32. Then
# TrackPopupMenuEx (win32k::menutrack, the window of the class #32768 in
# win32k::menupopup): the keys below, typed at each "[menu] tracking N" (the
# owner's first WM_ENTERIDLE), the mouse by messages W posts to the menu's
# window, EndMenu. Then the bar (win32k::menutrack): clicks by messages W
# sends and posts, the keys (Alt, Alt+letter, F10, the arrows, Escape), at
# last a real click on File, whose menu stays open for the capture.

requires = userland, native
initial-command = MENU1.EXE

timeout  = 120
