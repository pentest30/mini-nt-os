# The cursors (win32k::cursor; tests/native/cursor1.c, MinGW, linked to
# ReactOS's kernel32 and user32): CURSOR1.EXE takes the place of
# winlogon.exe and switches to WinSta0\Default, which has user32 load the
# 16 system cursors from its resources (the callback LOADDEFAULTCURSORS:
# CreateDC, CreateCompatibleBitmap, StretchDIBits, NtUserSetCursorIconData,
# NtUserSetSystemCursor) and make the arrow the thread's cursor.
# LoadCursor(NULL, IDC_*) finds them. Its window (WS_THICKFRAME, 200,200 to
# 1000,700) has the cross for its class cursor; the pointer, from the centre
# of the screen (640,400), goes into its client area, onto its left border
# (x 201) and back (x 423): WM_SETCURSOR each time, DefWindowProc taking the
# class cursor, then IDC_SIZEWE, then the cross again. The screen shows the
# cross at the pointer (423,405): its hot spot at its centre, its bars 8
# pixels each way (user32's resource), the white window elsewhere.
