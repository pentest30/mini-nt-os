# menus (shell32, CMenuToolbarBase::CreateToolbar) put their toolbar into a
# pager they create after it (SetParent, NtUserSetParent): without it, the
# toolbar stayed the pager's sibling, under it, and the submenu showed an
# empty box (the toolbar painted its items, all clipped by its sibling).
# The disk of `logon` (two boots: the setup by minoinit, then the logon of
# Administrator and explorer's desktop).
